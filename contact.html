Penn Rising XI was created to reduce fragmentation, keep more top players local, and provide a clearer development pathway across the region.
Families get a more consistent club identity, stronger pathway communication, and better alignment between age groups, coaching standards, and competitive platforms.
The long-term goal is to build stronger teams at every age, retain more regional talent, and create a stable home for elite development in the Greater Lehigh Valley.
Tryouts are the main entry point into the 2026-27 structure, helping place players into the right environment within one connected pathway.
