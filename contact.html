US Youth Soccer, and US Club Soccer have collectively decided to move to an age group player formation cycle that runs from August 1 to July 31, starting with the 2026-27 season/registration year.

Current System: Birth year (January 1 - December 31)

New System: Seasonal year (August 1 - July 31)

This is a nationwide change. ALL CLUBS will be transitioning to grade-based teams. ECNL, ECNL-RL, GA, GA-Aspire, DPL, EA, MLS Next Academy, and State Leagues. MLS HomeGrown is the only league that will operate on the Birth Year system

Penn Rising XI (PRXI) was formed through the consolidation of Lehigh Valley United (LVU) and HEX FC. Our goal is to create one unified club for Northeastern Pennsylvania with a clear pathway to the highest levels of the game.
Reduce player movement between clubs

Keep top players local to our area

Provide a clear, stable development pathway

Build stronger teams at every age group
One unified player pool from Quakertown and Upper Bucks to Northeastern PA

Clear pathway from youth to elite levels

Consistent coaching and standards across teams

Balanced approach to competition and development
We will be releasing coaching assignments soon. We will offer a robust staff of coaches from HEX FC and Lehigh Valley United to create a top level slate.

Our philosophy is centered on developing confident, effective decision-makers who compete with purpose in a player-centered, high-performance environment.
MLS NEXT Homegrown is the highest level of youth player development in North America.

It provides:

A 10-month, professional-style season

Competition against top academies across the country

National and international event opportunities

A direct pathway to college and professional soccer

Note: Players selected for this highest level are not allowed to also play high school soccer

Players train in a high-performance environment designed to prepare them for the next level.
MLS NEXT Academy is a high-level competitive platform that serves as a direct pathway into MLS NEXT Homegrown teams.

It provides:

Strong regional and national competition

A structured development environment

Clear progression opportunities within the club

Note: Players selected for this highest level are allowed to also play high school soccer
Penn Rising XI is building a complete pathway:

Local Development → Elite Competition → College and Professional Opportunities
