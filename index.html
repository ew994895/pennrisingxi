About The Club

One club built from LVU and HEX FC.

Penn Rising XI brings together Lehigh Valley United and HEX FC into one unified player development platform. The goal is straightforward: stronger player pools, stronger teams, and a clearer pathway for ambitious players who want a serious environment close to home.

The club is built for families looking for more than a patchwork experience. Training standards, team formation, recruiting support, and long-term development all sit inside one structure.

Real club photography gives the site immediate credibility and a more grounded academy feel.

Player Pathway

Built around development, competition, and recruiting.

The pathway should be easy for players and parents to understand at a glance. Each lane below uses club photography instead of generic filler panels.

Boys Pathway

Boys teams are positioned around high standards, demanding training, and a pathway that points toward elite competition. The structure is built to support real progression from younger development years into top performance environments.

Girls Pathway

The girls side is designed for players who want a stronger environment, a clear standard, and a platform that supports meaningful competition and long-term growth.

Youth Development

Younger age groups focus on technical comfort, game habits, and consistent coaching. The objective is to build players methodically, not rush them through the process.

College Recruitment

Older players need support beyond weekly sessions. Recruiting communication, exposure, and staff guidance are part of the pathway for families preparing for the next level.
