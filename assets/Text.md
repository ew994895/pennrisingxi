about/philosophy - 


Penn Rising XI represents a unified vision for the future of soccer in the Greater Lehigh Valley. PRXI is committed to player development, access, and long-term opportunity for all our athletes. For years, these clubs have independently developed high-level players while competing within the same regional landscape. By bringing these athletes together, Penn Rising XI transforms regional competition into collaboration, aligning coaching expertise, leadership, and infrastructure to better serve players in the Greater Lehigh Valley Area.

Our technical staff  has expectations of all its players.  Throughout the course of the year our players and teams should see growth in all facets of their game.  Our coaches will Inspire our players to improve using on field training, match play and video.  Competition, attitude and performance are our pillars of player/team growth.  

Technical Skills:  Technical skills are the foundation of a PR-XI player.  Each player is expected to train on his/her own.  

Physical Fitness:  PR-XI players are required to be physically fit.  Endurance, speed, agility, and strength are the building blocks to a successful athletic performance.  Coaches will promote healthy living habits.

Tactical Awareness:  PR-XI players will be able to read  and process the game which will best help the team achieve its objectives.   Great instincts, timing and proper Mentality: PR-XI players will be mentally tough to handle the rigors of both training and matches.  Being focused and composed, as well as having the resilience to overcome setbacks. Off the field, the use of film is a critical component of player development.  Players and coaches  must be committed to taking the time to break down film

Leadership:  Leadership is an essential component of A PR-XI player.  He/she must be able to inspire and motivate their teammates as well as set an example through their actions.  The ability to communicate and express thoughts and instructions are invaluable skills.

Passion:  Passion is a crucial element of our club as it fuels motivation, determination, and commitment. PR-XI players have a deep love of the game and a burning desire to succeed.

Commitment:  The backbone quality of PR-XI drives all successful players.  All PR-XI players must be willing to grind to grow personally and as a team.  This means making sacrifices and continuing ways to improve, regardless of outside circumstances.

Intangibles: Be a good person on and off the field.  High character individuals who positively contribute to our community are a staple for A PR-XI player.  Balancing a busy schedule and succeeding in the classroom. Players understand that their status will always be challenged both within and outside our club.

about/mission - 

our mission is simple. to develop exceptional student-athletes who are driven to compete at the highest levels of the game.

Based in Eastern Pennsylvania, we are committed to building a culture that cultivates elite talent, relentless work ethic, and a deep passion for soccer.

We strive to create a supportive, high-performance environment where motivated players can grow technically, tactically, physically, and mentally. Through professional coaching, competitive opportunities, and a team-first mindset, we empower our athletes to push their limits, embrace challenges, and pursue their ambitions at the collegiate, professional, and beyond.

Penn Rising XI is more than a club; it’s a community of dedicated players and families united by a shared standard of excellence, character, and the drive to rise together.

about/the badge - 

THE NAME

Penn: reflects our roots in Pennsylvania and our pride in representing the region’s strong soccer culture and community. 

Rising: symbolizes growth, ambition, and the relentless pursuit of improvement.

XI: represents the eleven players who take the field as one unit. It reflects teamwork, unity, and the belief that success comes from collective effort, trust, and commitment to the group.

Together, Penn Rising XI stands for a club built on regional pride, continuous development, and a team-first mentality. Where dedicated athletes come to rise, compete, and reach the highest levels of the game.

Suggests momentum, elevation, and the idea of players rising to higher levels.

US Youth Soccer, and US Club Soccer have collectively decided to move to an age group player formation cycle that runs from August 1 to July 31, starting with the 2026-27 season/registration year. Our associations feel that this age group player formation will be the most inclusive and help increase participation. - US Soccer

This is a nationwide change. ALL CLUBS will be transitioning to grade-based teams. ECNL, ECNL-RL, GA, GA-Aspire, DPL, EA, MLS Next Academy, and State Leagues. MLS HomeGrown is the only league that will operate on the Birth Year system
