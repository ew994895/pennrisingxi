For the 2026-27 soccer season PR XI is operating on the new Seasonal Year age group categorization. This mean all team will be formed in their respective age group based on date of births that fall between August 1 and July 31.

*MLS HomeGrown Teams WILL operate by birth year but please register at the correct seasonal year - PRXI will internally identify players for HG teams and follow up
