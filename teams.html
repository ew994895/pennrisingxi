Teams

The club is organized to support players through foundation, development, and elite competition years.

Foundation Phase

U8–U10 players focus on technical habits, confidence, and structured training.

Development Phase

U11–U12 players continue to build game understanding and competitive habits.

Elite Phase

U13–U19 players move through higher levels of competition and recruiting preparation.

Platform Direction

The club structure is aligned toward stronger long-term opportunities on both the boys and girls sides.
