the badge

The mark stands for a single identity across the club: unified standards, upward ambition, and a commitment to building something lasting in the region.

one crest for one club

The local site previously used the badge mostly as a decorative asset. This page reframes it as part of the broader club story, closer to how the live site organizes its brand pages.

  • The badge signals a unified player pool and a shared future.
  • The visual identity is meant to feel bold, direct, and regionally rooted.
  • Semper Unum reinforces the club’s central idea: always one.
