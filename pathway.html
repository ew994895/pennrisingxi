Player Pathway

Clear progression from early years to elite competition.

Penn Rising XI is built to give players and families a visible pathway through development years, top competition levels, and recruiting ages.

Youth Development: U8–U12

These years center on technical quality, repetition, confidence on the ball, and learning the game in a demanding but age-appropriate environment.

Performance Years: U13–U19

As players get older, expectations sharpen around performance, role clarity, recruiting support, and readiness for stronger competition.

Boys

MLS NEXT direction

The boys side is positioned around elite standards and stronger competitive planning, with a pathway designed to move serious players higher.

  • Technical and tactical progression
  • Clearer performance expectations
  • Better alignment across age groups
Girls

Competitive platform with long-term vision

The girls side is built to support serious players through strong team environments, meaningful competition, and consistent developmental standards.

  • Serious training environment
  • Higher competitive standards
  • Support for future recruiting steps
