mission

The mission is to build one club structure with clearer development standards, stronger competition platforms, and more continuity for families.

Keep Players Local

Fewer families should feel forced to leave the region.

The club is designed to hold more talent in the Greater Lehigh Valley by improving the quality and clarity of the pathway available here.

Clear Pathway

Every age group should connect to the next one.

From foundation years through elite teams and college recruiting, the structure should feel connected instead of improvised.

Consistent Standards

Coaching and expectations should scale together.

Players need continuity in training demands, communication, and competitive environment as they move up.

Pathway

one mission across every phase

Foundation

U8-U10

Build confidence, technical comfort, and the habits that support real development later.

Development

U11-U12

Raise tactical understanding and decision-making without losing the emphasis on technical quality.

Pre-Elite

U13-U15

Increase competitive demands and prepare players for more selective roster environments.

Elite

U16-U19

Compete at the highest appropriate platform with clearer support around exposure and recruiting.
