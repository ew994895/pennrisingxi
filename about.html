About Penn Rising XI

Penn Rising XI was created to bring LVU and HEX FC into one stronger club structure for players and families in Eastern Pennsylvania.

A more unified structure for development and competition.

Penn Rising XI brings together Lehigh Valley United and HEX FC to create a single player pool and a clearer pathway across age groups. The objective is practical: reduce fragmentation, improve team formation, and provide a more stable environment for player development.

The club is designed to give families clearer information, stronger standards, and better alignment between youth development, elite competition, and recruiting years.

Foundation

LVU and HEX FC come together

Structure

Pathways are aligned across age groups

Players and families have a clearer picture of how the club is organized.

Direction

Competition platforms continue to strengthen

The club is built to support stronger long-term opportunities for players in the region.
