About / Our Story

One club. One player pool. One standard.

Penn Rising XI was formed to bring LVU and HEX FC into one stronger structure for players and families across the Greater Lehigh Valley.

Why It Matters

A clearer development platform for the region.

The purpose of Penn Rising XI is simple. Top players in Eastern Pennsylvania should not need a scattered experience to find a serious environment. By bringing the two clubs together, Penn Rising XI creates stronger age groups, stronger internal competition, and a more stable structure for long-term development.

The club is designed to support players from youth development years through older recruiting ages while keeping standards consistent across the pathway.

Unified Club

Stronger player pools

Combining LVU and HEX FC gives the club a better platform for team formation and player progression across age groups.

Development

Consistent standards

Training quality, expectations, and communication should feel connected from one age group to the next.

Region

Keep top players local

The club is built to give ambitious players a trusted option close to home instead of fragmenting the area’s talent.

Timeline

How the club is taking shape.

Foundation

LVU and HEX FC come together

The club begins with a clear objective: create one identity and one pathway that can serve the region at a higher level.

Pathway

Boys and girls structures are aligned

Program planning becomes easier to communicate when player development, competition, and recruiting support sit under one standard.

Next Step

Elite platforms become central

MLS NEXT, Girls Academy direction, and a stronger overall environment become clearer parts of the club’s identity.
